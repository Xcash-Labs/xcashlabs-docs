# Introducing XCash Klassic (XCK)

*Published May 2026*

## A New Chapter for the Original X-CASH Blockchain

XCash Klassic (XCK) is an independent continuation and revitalization of the original X-CASH blockchain ecosystem.

The project was created to preserve and modernize the original network while remaining committed to the principles that inspired its creation: privacy, decentralization, community participation, and long-term sustainability.

Built upon the original CryptoNote and Monero-based foundation, XCash Klassic focuses on maintaining a privacy-first digital cash network while improving infrastructure, documentation, governance, tooling, and user experience.

## Core Principles

XCash Klassic is guided by several core principles:

- Privacy as a fundamental right
- Open-source and transparent development
- Fair and accessible participation
- Community-driven governance
- Long-term sustainability and network stability

## Fair Distribution

XCash Klassic continues the original network's commitment to fair distribution.

The network launched without:

- Premine
- Airdrops
- Founder allocations
- Special token distributions

Participation in the network remains open to all users under the same rules.

## Technology Foundation

XCash Klassic builds upon the technology and concepts originally introduced by the X-CASH ecosystem, including:

- CryptoNote privacy technology
- Monero-derived blockchain architecture
- Delegated Proof of Private Stake (DPoPS)
- Reserve-proof-based voting
- Verifiable randomness mechanisms
- Community governance participation

XCash Labs continues to modernize and refine these systems while maintaining compatibility with the project's long-term vision.

## XCash Labs

XCash Labs is leading the revitalization effort and supports ongoing development, infrastructure, documentation, wallet software, and ecosystem growth.

Development is conducted openly through public repositories, community discussion, and transparent release processes.
